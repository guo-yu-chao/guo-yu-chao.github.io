# About Me

I am Yu-Chao Guo, a Ph.D. student at Xi'an Jiaotong University.

I received my B.Sc. degree in Chemistry from Northeast Agricultural University and my M.Sc. degree from the State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials, Jilin University, under the supervision of Professor Wenke Zhang.

My current research focuses on polymer mechanochemistry, particularly the regulation of mechanophore activation through polymer network topology engineering for tough elastomeric materials.

## Research Interests

- Polymer Mechanochemistry
- Network Topology Engineering
- Toughening of Elastomers
