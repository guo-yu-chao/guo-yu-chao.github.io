# About Me

I am **Yu-Chao Guo (国玉超)**, a Ph.D. student at the School of Chemical Engineering and Technology, **Xi'an Jiaotong University**, under the supervision of **Prof. Hang Zhang (张航教授)**.

I received my M.Sc. degree in Chemistry from the **State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials, Jilin University** (2023 – 2026), under the supervision of **Prof. Wenke Zhang (张文科教授)**. Prior to that, I completed my B.Sc. degree in Chemistry at **Northeast Agricultural University** (2019 – 2023).

My research focuses on **polymer mechanochemistry** and **polymer network topology engineering**, aiming to understand stress propagation mechanisms and force-induced mechanophore activation to design high-performance, tough elastomeric materials.
