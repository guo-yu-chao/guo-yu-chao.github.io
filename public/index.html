Biography

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Chemical Engineering and Technology, Xi'an Jiaotong University. Prior to joining XJTU, I received my M.Sc. degree from the State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials at Jilin University under the guidance of Prof. Wenke Zhang, and my B.Sc. degree in Chemistry from Northeast Agricultural University.

My research lies at the intersection of polymer chemistry, mechanochemistry, and materials topology. Specifically, I am interested in designing novel elastomeric networks where polymer network topology controls force transmission to regulate mechanophore activation, enabling high-performance and resilient materials.

Research Thrusts

Polymer Mechanochemistry

Elucidating force-induced chemical transformations and bond cleavage in polymeric architectures under mechanical stress.

Network Topology Engineering

Tailoring chain entanglements, crosslink distribution, and spatial architecture to manipulate stress distribution.

Toughening of Elastomers

Developing tough, damage-reporting, and self-healing elastomeric materials via mechanochemical strategies.
