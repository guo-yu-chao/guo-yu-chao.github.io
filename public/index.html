About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Chemical Engineering and Technology, Xi'an Jiaotong University. Prior to joining XJTU, I obtained my Master's degree from the State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials at Jilin University under the supervision of Prof. Wenke Zhang.

My research focuses on polymer mechanochemistry and polymer network topology engineering. I am particularly interested in understanding how force transmission across topological networks regulates mechanophore activation to design high-performance, tough, and functional elastomeric materials.
