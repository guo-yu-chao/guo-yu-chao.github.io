About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Chemical Engineering and Technology, Xi'an Jiaotong University, under the supervision of Prof. Hang Zhang (张航教授).

Prior to joining XJTU, I obtained my Master's degree in Chemistry from the State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials at Jilin University (2023–2026) under the supervision of Prof. Wenke Zhang (张文科教授), and my B.Sc. degree in Chemistry from Northeast Agricultural University (2019–2023).

My research focuses on polymer mechanochemistry and polymer network topology engineering. I am particularly interested in understanding force transmission across macromolecular topological networks and regulating mechanophore activation to design high-performance, resilient, and tough elastomeric materials.

Research Areas

Polymer Mechanochemistry

Understanding force-induced chemical bond cleavage and mechanophore activation pathways in polymeric architectures.

Polymer Network Topology Engineering

Manipulating crosslink structures, entanglements, and network spatial architecture to dictate stress distribution.

Mechanochemical Toughening & Resilient Elastomers

Developing resilient, damage-reporting, and self-healing elastomeric materials via mechanochemical and topology strategies.
