About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Chemical Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University. I received my B.Sc. degree in Chemistry from Northeast Agricultural University and my M.Sc. degree from the State Key Laboratory of Supramolecular Structure and Materials, Jilin University, under the supervision of Prof. Wenke Zhang.

My current research focuses on polymer mechanochemistry, specifically investigating how polymer network topology regulates force transmission and mechanophore activation for designing high-performance, tough elastomeric materials.
